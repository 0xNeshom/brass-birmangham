# The Calder Marches — Industry Registry

Status — Phase 1 distribution proposal. These districts describe the world and its reusable visual vocabulary. They define no playable slots, production quantities, costs or tournament rules.

Related documents — [City registry](WORLD_CITY_REGISTRY.md), [geography](WORLD_GEOGRAPHY_SPEC.md), [routes](WORLD_ROUTE_DESIGN.md), [asset manifest](WORLD_ASSET_MANIFEST.md).

## Distribution principles

Place extraction close to the fictional resource seams, water-driven mills at reliable falls, heavy works on broad industrial ground, and freight stores at engineered transport interfaces. The central basin combines ironstone and limestone with coal imported from the northern uplands. Copperfield’s copper is imported metal stock. Agricultural processing sits near barley and clean tributary water in the south. Freight, rather than a new mine, explains the eastern ports.

## Cross-region goods relationships

The coal chain runs from Brackenford and Blackridge through Calderwick to the basin. Ironhaven and Foundry Vale turn that fuel and local mineral inputs into metal. Copperfield finishes machinery from the basin’s output. Millbrook combines western wool and port imports with river power. Dunmere supplies beer and grain to Ashbourne and the industrial cities. Greyhaven consolidates inland cargo and Rivermouth exchanges it with the external coast.

No district needs an identical cluster of all industry types. Twelve primary district identities keep the overview readable. Secondary small workshops may be part of a future building illustration but must not be mistaken for additional canonical industry locations.

## Representation and future separation

Use one primary district marker per listed district in the Phase 1 concept, with an offset label if needed. Markers are geographical anchors rather than full building footprints. A later registry can describe surveyed footprints after composition approval. Day uses engraved roof groups and restrained brass highlights. Night adds a few warm windows and furnace mouths at the same district locations. A light color change cannot create or remove a district.
