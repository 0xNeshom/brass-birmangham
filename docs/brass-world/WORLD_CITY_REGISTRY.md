## Label and review rules

The registry defines city centers as settlement identities. Industry yards, stations, quays and bridges have their own IDs and may be offset from those centers when the composition is approved. A city receives one stable ID in both environments. A label adjustment does not alter its identity or imply a geographical move.

The candidate names are fictional in their use here. Names such as Ashbourne and Calder may also occur in real places. Their presence does not authorize borrowing the layout of those places. No candidate name has been changed.
